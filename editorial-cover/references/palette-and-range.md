# Palette And Range

Avoid collapsing every cover into cream, warm gray, brown, female portrait, and giant serif type. Choose the palette that supports the subject.

## Quiet Neutral

Use for lifestyle, architecture, solitude, work, travel, and slow living.

- ivory, warm gray, charcoal, olive, concrete, soft daylight
- large serif or clean sans
- restrained contrast and generous negative space

## Cool Technology

Use for AI, data, identity, future, synthetic beauty, or digital society.

- blue gray, silver, skin tone, white, cold teal, pale cyan
- thin geometric sans, outlined type, grid lines
- clinical light, close crop, precise surfaces

## High-Contrast Social Issue

Use for consumption, platform life, generational tension, climate, urban pressure, or cultural critique.

- black/white, paper white, hard shadow, deep green, muted red only as accent
- bold sans or sharp serif
- visual metaphor should be legible in one second

## Warm Lifestyle

Use for routine, wellness, home, calm productivity, reading, food, travel, or personal ritual.

- morning yellow, wood, cream, soft orange, pale blue, plant green
- large serif with small clean sans
- natural light and tactile materials

## Clean Beauty / Product

Use for beauty, skincare, fragrance, wellness, premium e-commerce, and product launches.

- powder blue, porcelain, ivory, glass, chrome, soft skin tone
- elegant serif or quiet modern sans
- product or face should have clean shape and enough air for type

## Rule

Pick one palette family and one accent. Do not combine all palettes in one cover.
