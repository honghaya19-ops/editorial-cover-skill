# Example Assets

These local examples calibrate taste and range. Use them as directional references, not as templates to copy exactly.

## `assets/examples/synthetic-skin.png`

Good for tech, identity, beauty, AI, and body-as-interface themes.

- close crop creates intimacy
- thin outlined sans matches the coded-skin concept
- grid and face lines form one visual system
- cool palette keeps the image quiet rather than cyberpunk

## `assets/examples/generational-media.png`

Good for social platform, media, generational tension, and public-space concepts.

- two figures create a clear comparison
- interface and newspaper form the visual metaphor
- headline is readable and integrated into the scene
- real platform logos are avoided

## `assets/examples/unboxed-commerce.png`

Good for consumption, e-commerce, minimal product critique, and social issue editorial.

- bold title crosses the subject instead of floating separately
- cardboard boxes and repeated wall text carry the concept
- white room keeps the message sharp

## `assets/examples/unfinished-architecture.png`

Good for architecture, design, fashion, structure, and unfinished-system themes.

- environment is the main visual device
- title sits in architectural negative space
- figure and building share the same formal rhythm

The original assets `quiet-structures.png`, `still-morning.png`, and `readme-hero.webp` remain useful for quiet lifestyle and architectural solitude. Add broader examples when new strong outputs appear.
